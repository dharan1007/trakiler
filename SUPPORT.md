# FORM Support, Feedback and Research Discussion

FORM is an independent research/side project. Public feedback is useful when it is specific, reproducible and separates software behavior from medical or coaching claims.

## Try FORM

Live app: https://dharan1007.github.io/trakiler/

## Where to report things

- **Software bug:** open a GitHub issue using the Bug Report form.
- **Research / validation concern:** use the Research & Validation Feedback issue form.
- **Feature idea:** use the Feature Request form.
- **Security or privacy vulnerability:** follow `SECURITY.md` instead of posting sensitive details publicly.
- **General product/research discussion:** email dharan.poduvu@gmail.com.

## Useful bug reports include

- browser and operating system;
- exact FORM screen/feature;
- steps to reproduce;
- expected versus observed behavior;
- whether the issue occurs in a clean/private browser session;
- screenshots only when they do not expose personal training or account data.

## Useful research feedback includes

- the exact metric, rule, formula, ranking or recommendation being questioned;
- the population/context where you believe it may fail;
- a DOI, PubMed link, journal citation or other primary source when available;
- whether the concern is scientific validity, UX interpretation, measurement error, bias, safety framing or terminology.

## Scope

FORM is not a medical service, injury-diagnosis system, clinical decision tool or guarantee of outcomes. Do not submit emergencies or highly sensitive medical information through GitHub issues.
