EXECUTION MANUAL

The rules that make the program work.

Use these standards to control fatigue, preserve joint position and make each logged repetition comparable to the last.

01

Rep quality

Lower under control for roughly two seconds. Stabilize the stretched position without bouncing. Drive the concentric with controlled intent.

02

RIR accuracy

One RIR means one more clean repetition was available. Stop when the next rep would require a changed path, shortened range or assistance.

03

Rest periods

Use 150–210 seconds for demanding compounds, 120 seconds for secondary machine work and 75–120 seconds for isolation movements.

04

Double progression

Reach the top of the assigned rep range across every set at the prescribed RIR, then increase load by the smallest practical increment.

05

Volume control

If performance falls in two consecutive exposures, remove 20–30% of that muscle's weekly sets. More fatigue is not automatically more stimulus.

06

Deload

Every sixth week, halve working sets, retain roughly 80–90% of normal load, leave three to four RIR and remove failure work.

STOP CONDITIONS

Pain is not a progression metric.

Stop the session for sharp pain, chest pressure, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, palpitations or neurological symptoms. Seek appropriate medical assessment rather than training through them.
