Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 August 2026

FORM is a free, independent side project. It is designed to collect and process only information needed to provide the features a user chooses. FORM has no advertising SDK, no sale of personal data, no third-party marketing profile, and no wearable or health-device integration.

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes the FORM workout tracker, optional cloud sync, optional body tracking and optional FORM Club leaderboard. It does not cover YouTube or other external sites opened through links.

2. Local-only data

FORM can function with data stored only in your browser. Local data can include your training profile, active program, workout sets, loads, repetitions, RIR, notes, completed workouts, bodyweight, body-fat entries you choose to enter, circumference measurements and local Club preview data. Local-only records are not uploaded by FORM unless you enable a cloud-backed feature.

3. Cloud data

When the FORM backend is configured and you use cloud sync, FORM may process: a pseudonymous account identifier; optional display name; training goal, experience level, weekly schedule and equipment preference; workout and set history; optional bodyweight/body-fat logs; optional circumference measurements; legal-policy acceptance versions; and private daily activity data used to calculate Club scores.

You do not need to provide an email for an anonymous FORM identity. If you voluntarily add an email for magic-link recovery, the authentication provider processes that email for login and account recovery.

4. Public Club data

Club publication is opt-in. If you enable Public Club profile and publish activity, the public leaderboard may display your chosen display name and daily aggregate score, completed sets, steps you manually entered, active minutes you manually entered, streak and total training volume. FORM does not intentionally publish your exact bodyweight, body measurements, private workout notes, email address or private set history.

5. Why data is used

FORM uses data only to operate requested app functionality: saving and synchronizing workouts, calculating progression and personal records, generating program or workload suggestions, showing body-tracking trends, authenticating your account, calculating transparent Club rankings, exporting your data and processing deletion requests. FORM does not use your training data for advertising, data brokerage or unrelated marketing.

6. Infrastructure processing

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When cloud features are enabled, authentication and database services are provided by Supabase. The browser also downloads the Supabase JavaScript client from a public package CDN. These infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps and security logs as part of hosting, delivery, abuse prevention and service operation. Those provider-level logs are not used by FORM to build advertising profiles.

7. Analytics and tracking

FORM does not include a product analytics SDK, behavioral advertising tracker, ad network, fingerprinting system or cross-site tracking pixel. If this changes materially, this policy should be updated before the new processing is enabled.

8. Retention

Browser data remains until you delete it, clear site storage or your browser removes it. Cloud app records remain until you delete your cloud account or the service is discontinued and its data is removed. Hosting, security logs and provider backups can have separate retention controlled by the infrastructure provider.

9. Your controls

  • Edit your training profile at any time.
  • Keep Club private or opt in to public ranking.
  • Export your local and, when connected, cloud data in JSON.
  • Delete local browser data separately.
  • Delete the cloud account through FORM's account-deletion flow; the backend is designed to delete the authentication identity and cascade deletion through FORM-owned user tables.
  • Stop using optional bodyweight or measurement tracking without affecting core workout logging.

10. Security

FORM's backend design uses row-level security so authenticated users can access their own private records, a deliberately reduced public leaderboard projection, server-side score calculation, and a server-only credential for account deletion. No browser file should contain a Supabase service-role key. No internet service can promise absolute security, so avoid putting highly sensitive medical information into free-text workout notes.

11. Children

FORM is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed to knowingly create accounts for children or to request parental-consent data.

12. International use

FORM is accessible on the internet and infrastructure providers can process data in multiple locations. Mandatory privacy rights in your jurisdiction continue to apply. The project is designed around data minimization, purpose limitation, access/export and deletion controls; this policy is not a legal certification for every jurisdiction.

13. Policy changes

Material privacy changes should be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, requesting fresh acceptance inside FORM before new processing begins.

14. Contact

FORM is maintained through the project repository. For non-sensitive technical questions, use the repository's normal contact channels. Do not post private health, account or identity information in a public issue.
